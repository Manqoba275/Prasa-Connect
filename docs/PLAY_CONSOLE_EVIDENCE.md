# Google Play Console readiness evidence

The lecturer has approved screenshots of the Play Console developer-account setup up to the registration-fee screen as evidence of publication preparation. Stop at the fee screen. Do not enter card details, add a payment method, or submit payment. This demonstrates account-registration progress only; it is not an active developer account, an uploaded app, or a published Play Store listing.

Google's official signup guidance requires an eligible Google account holder, acceptance of the Developer Distribution Agreement during registration, and a one-time registration fee. The exact screens and verification steps can vary by account type and may change. Follow the current prompts shown by Google; do not invent or recreate screenshots. See [Get started with Play Console](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435) and [required account information](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312).

## Before signup

- Have the group agree who is the account owner and who is authorised to act for the group. The account holder must use their own Google account and accurate legal/contact information; do not create an organisation account unless there is a real eligible organisation.
- Sign in to the intended Google account and open [Play Console signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup).
- Keep personal identity information, verification documents and payment details out of GitHub, this repository, the recording and shared screenshots. Save evidence in the lecturer's approved private submission location.
- Prepare the app evidence separately: current app icon, store screenshots, app description, privacy-policy URL, data-safety answers, content-rating answers, package name, version details, and a release-signed APK/AAB. A debug APK is not a release artifact. Never share or commit the signing keystore or its passwords.
